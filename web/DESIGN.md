# Wazalendo Design

## Direction

An editorial wildlife field guide with a full-bleed, natural-color daylight lion hero, dark transparent text shading, and restrained lime accents. Keep the sighting records readable and data-led beneath the cinematic opening.

## Tokens

- Background: paper `#F4F5EC`
- Surface: white `#FFFFFF`
- Text: charcoal `#191C16`
- Accent: lime `#B3D72F`
- Deep accent: olive `#58740B`
- Display lettering: Barlow Condensed
- Reading text: DM Sans
- Editorial accent: Playfair Display
- Small radius: `6px`; medium radius: `12px`

## Motion

The locally hosted lion video plays muted by default; the roar control seeks to the open-mouth moment and enables its audio after a user click. The motion control pauses the video and ambient particles. Respect reduced-motion preferences. An Africa outline overlays the hero with selectable country lights aggregated from loaded sighting coordinates.

## Page

Keep the home page as the primary experience: a full-viewport lion hero, honest loaded-record counts, searchable/filterable sighting cards, and the Wazalendo context section. Use clear keyboard focus, touch-sized controls, and responsive artwork placement.
